# E7.83 — Governed Collaboration Lifecycle Closure & Contract Retirement

## Objective
Retire completed collaboration contracts into durable evidence without deleting history or creating new authority.

## Invariants
Closure requires a verification reference and evidence. Reopened/blocked contracts are not retired. Retirement never grants execution authority. A successor contract may be referenced without mutating the retired record.
