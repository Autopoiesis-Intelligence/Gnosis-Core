# E8.12 — Partnership Proposal Generator

## Objective
Generate an evidence-bound commercial partnership proposal only after an admitted commercial evidence decision.

## Required content
Partner scope, minimal core scope, expected result, constraints, evidence references and commercial decision reference.

## Invariants
The generator cannot bypass the commercial evidence gate, create a contract or grant execution authority. The generated proposal remains subject to review/acceptance.
