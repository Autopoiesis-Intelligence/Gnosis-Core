# E7.91 — Candidate Contract Generation Boundary

## Objective
Transform accepted patterns and relations into explicit candidate contracts for shadow evaluation, without directly modifying or authorizing the Core.

## Invariants
Candidates are deterministically bound to extraction, pattern/relation references and evidence. A candidate must have an explicit objective and scope. Proposed candidates enter shadow evaluation; candidate generation never grants execution authority or commits a change.
