# E8.15 — Partner Core Transfer Boundary

## Objective
Define the exact boundary of what may be transferred to a partner while excluding internal AI context and Research-Memory.

## Manifest
Minimal core artifacts, evidence artifacts, explicit exclusions, partner scope and transfer policy.

## Invariants
Internal AI_CONTEXT and Research-Memory cannot be listed as transferable core artifacts. Every transfer requires explicit exclusions and an approved manifest plus a ready contract. The transfer boundary never grants execution authority.
