# E8.00 — Opportunity Value Grid & Contract Routing

## Objective
Provide a deterministic value grid for opportunities discovered by Self-Learning, ranging from low-relevance backlog items through research-relevant work to commercial partner opportunities.

## Levels
- LOW_RELEVANCE: retain as general backlog; no implied priority or execution authority.
- RESEARCH_RELEVANT: route to a research contract and evidence workflow.
- COMMERCIAL: route to a partner contract scope and isolated commercial learning boundary.

## Invariants
Every classification requires source and evidence references, explicit scope, rationale and status. Classification is routing metadata, not execution authority. Commercial opportunities require a partner-scoped destination before contract formation.
