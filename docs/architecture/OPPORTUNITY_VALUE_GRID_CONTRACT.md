# E8.00 — Opportunity Value Grid & Contract Routing

## Priority model

The opportunity grid has three value priorities:

1. **LOW_RELEVANCE** — currently low practical relevance, but with identifiable latent or additional value that may be developed through further evidence, relations or research. It remains in the general backlog.
2. **RESEARCH_RELEVANT** — sufficiently relevant for structured research and legal/contractual framing, but without an established financial component. It routes to a research contract.
3. **COMMERCIAL** — an opportunity where a financial/economic component is already present or explicitly evidenced. It routes to a partner-scoped commercial contract.

The levels describe opportunity value and routing priority; they do not constitute a quality ranking or execution authority.

## Invariants
Every classification requires source and evidence references, explicit scope, rationale and status. Commercial classification requires evidence of the financial component and a partner-scoped destination before commercial contract formation.
