# E7.73 — Governed Feedback Promotion Record

## Objective
Record the exact evidence and target knowledge revision for a partner feedback promotion after validation.

## Required provenance
Validation ID, proposal ID, source delivery receipt, target knowledge revision, promoted findings, exclusions and decision are mandatory.

## Rule
The record is valid only for a PROMOTE validation decision and a PROMOTED decision record.

## Boundary
This record does not mutate the Core by itself. It records the governed promotion result and its provenance.

## Status
PARTIAL / UNVERIFIED.
