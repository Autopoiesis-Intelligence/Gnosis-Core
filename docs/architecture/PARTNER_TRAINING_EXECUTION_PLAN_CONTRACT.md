# E7.65 — Partner Training Execution Plan

## Objective
Convert an authorized Partner Training Intake into a bounded machine-readable learning plan.

## Required controls
The plan explicitly declares allowed data references, learning objectives, Core invariant references, test requirements, acceptance criteria and revision.

## Safety
A plan cannot authorize itself, alter Core invariants, or expand the source-data boundary. Data references must be a subset of the authorized intake scope.

## Acceptance
A specialized Core cannot be delivered merely because a plan exists. Its acceptance criteria and evidence must be satisfied through the governed execution path.
