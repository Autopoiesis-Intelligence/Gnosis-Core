# E7.64 — Partner Training Intake

## Objective
Bind an incoming partner contract to a bounded training request before any partner data enters the Self-Learning pipeline.

## Required identity
Partner ID, contract ID, domain, knowledge scope, source references, constraints and requested revision are mandatory.

## Trust boundary
Authorization is required for both the partner identity and knowledge scope. The intake is PROPOSED and cannot itself grant execution authority or modify Core.

## Privacy
Partner source references remain scoped to the authorized partner knowledge boundary. Cross-partner sharing requires a separate governed promotion path.
