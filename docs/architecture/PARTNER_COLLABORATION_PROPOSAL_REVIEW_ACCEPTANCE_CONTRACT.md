# E7.75 — Governed Collaboration Proposal Review & Acceptance Contract

## Objective

Define the governed boundary between a machine-generated collaboration proposal and an accepted collaboration instruction.

E7.75 applies to proposals generated by E7.74 or equivalent reviewed proposal artifacts.

This contract does **not** publish to GitHub, invite collaborators, grant repository permissions, expose private data, mutate Ψ-Core, or execute external actions.

## Scope

A collaboration proposal may describe:

- collaboration mode (COMMERCIAL or OPEN);
- target repository/project scope;
- requested inputs;
- proposed deliverables;
- contract/evidence dependencies;
- publication target;
- privacy and disclosure constraints;
- requested human/owner review;
- expiration or revision conditions.

The review layer determines whether the proposal is:

- ACCEPTED;
- REJECTED;
- DEFERRED;
- RETURNED_FOR_REVISION.

Acceptance is a governance record, not an execution authorization.

## Non-goals

This contract MUST NOT:

- publish a repository, issue, PR, package, document or social announcement;
- send invitations or grant GitHub permissions;
- reveal partner-private or user-private data;
- mutate Core state or Core invariants;
- activate capabilities;
- create execution authority merely by proposal generation or acceptance;
- treat silence, import, registry presence or proposal existence as acceptance.

## Required provenance

A review record MUST bind:

1. exact proposal identity/digest;
2. proposal revision;
3. generator/source revision;
4. referenced contract revisions;
5. dependency/evidence references;
6. collaboration target and scope;
7. privacy/disclosure classification;
8. reviewer identity;
9. review decision;
10. review reason;
11. review timestamp or monotonic evidence identifier;
12. resulting revision/status.

Where a referenced artifact has a content digest, the digest MUST be retained.

## Acceptance rules

### ACCEPTED

A proposal MAY be marked ACCEPTED only when:

- the proposal is structurally valid;
- required dependencies are present;
- requested scope is explicit;
- privacy/disclosure constraints are explicit;
- required evidence references are available;
- no known prohibition is violated;
- the reviewer has authority to record the decision.

ACCEPTED means:

> the proposal is eligible to proceed to a separate execution-authorization boundary.

It does not mean that execution is authorized.

### REJECTED

REJECTED proposals remain historical evidence and MUST NOT be silently deleted or rewritten.

### DEFERRED

DEFERRED preserves the proposal as unresolved and MUST identify the missing evidence, decision or condition where available.

### RETURNED_FOR_REVISION

Returned proposals require a new revision or explicit revision linkage before they may be reviewed again.

## Separation of authority

The following distinctions are mandatory:

Proposal generation != Proposal review
Proposal review != Execution authorization
Execution authorization != External execution
Execution != Execution evidence
Execution evidence != Core authority

No review record may be interpreted as permission to:

- write to GitHub;
- invite a user;
- alter repository settings;
- publish private material;
- execute code;
- mutate Core.

## Revision and replay

A review decision is bound to the exact proposal revision.

A changed proposal MUST NOT inherit ACCEPTED status implicitly.

Exact replay of the same proposal/review inputs MAY reproduce the same deterministic review-record identity.

A conflicting replay MUST fail closed.

A superseding proposal revision MUST preserve linkage to the prior proposal and its review outcome.

## Privacy boundary

Private partner/user material MUST remain outside the collaboration proposal unless the proposal explicitly references an authorized shareable abstraction.

A collaboration proposal MUST NOT be used as a covert transport for private payloads.

Publication targets do not authorize disclosure.

## Evidence requirements

The review layer MUST preserve enough evidence to reconstruct:

- what was proposed;
- what revision was reviewed;
- what contracts/evidence were relied upon;
- who recorded the decision;
- why the decision was made;
- what scope was accepted;
- whether the decision remains current.

Review evidence is non-authoritative with respect to Core mutation.

## Failure behavior

Malformed, incomplete, stale, revoked, scope-expanding or provenance-conflicting proposals MUST NOT become ACCEPTED.

Unknown authority or ambiguous privacy state MUST resolve to non-acceptance pending explicit review.

No partial acceptance may silently broaden scope.

## Acceptance gate

E7.75 is considered satisfied only when an implementation and tests demonstrate:

1. deterministic review-record construction;
2. exact proposal revision binding;
3. explicit ACCEPTED/REJECTED/DEFERRED/RETURNED_FOR_REVISION states;
4. rejection of stale or conflicting proposal replay;
5. preservation of rejected/deferred history;
6. privacy/scope validation;
7. separation from external execution authorization;
8. exact-commit runtime/CI evidence.

Documentation alone is not implementation proof.
