# E7.80 — Governed Remediation Plan & Compensation Authorization

A remediation plan converts a resolved incident into a bounded proposed response.

## Invariants
- Evidence and preconditions are mandatory.
- Accepted plans may be admitted to the next authorization gate but do not grant execution authority.
- Manual review cannot become an accepted mutation plan.
- Privacy classification and scope remain explicit.
